# Visual system and artwork

Open the original design boards in `assets/visual-reference` when selecting layouts. `customer-device-detail.png` shows new, used and QC report hierarchy. `customer-repair-components.png` shows reusable repair controls. `vendor-profile.png` shows the identity, stats, plan, and settings stack. Compare latest source for navigation or actions that evolved after the boards.

| Role | Original tokens and patterns |
|---|---|

Plus Jakarta Sans gives larger headings and action labels their weight. Some home pieces use Poppins and parts of search use their own tokens. Use existing artwork through `ui_kit.py list ROLE` and `show ROLE art:FOLDER`. Customer loader assets include device, bag, cart and delivery props. Customer feature families include `repair`, `exchange`, `sell`, `orders`, `cart`, `profile`, `shops` and `auction`. Vendor `assets/icons/` carries quick-action art, `assets/categories/` device cutouts, and `assets/3d/` the feature illustrations. The layout consumes raster 3D-style cutouts. Keep the original alpha and fit each image with `BoxFit.contain` where the full silhouette conveys meaning.

When new art is requested, use the matching bundled asset as the visual reference. For a quick action, specify one recognizable object, centered three-quarter view, soft light from the upper left, rounded volume, restrained specular highlights, and isolated transparent background with even padding. Use no embedded label unless the brief calls for typography inside the art. Check it at 46 px against the actual light or dark surface. For a hero, plan a safe text region and keep product photography visually separate from UI labels. Review at crop sizes, including the smallest supported phone width.

Keep motion local. The original vendor `Art3D` floats by roughly five logical pixels on a 2.6 s cycle, with a small rotation. Respect reduced motion. Match rounded surfaces and transitions by preserving clip, border, shadow and motion values from the selected source. Keep the card image’s decode size near its displayed size. Use horizontal lists for shelves, bounded aspect ratios for banners, and native tap targets for controls. Test scrolling, rapid switches, two-line names, larger text, sparse results, sold-out items, and media errors at the intended device size.

Future reuse starts with the existing component and its input contract. Keep actual data and accessibility text outside the artwork. Adapt the brand through tokens, logos, copy and product assets that the destination is authorized to use.
